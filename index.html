    Eraser Types

  • Pixel Eraser
    Erases individual pixels
    Erases the exact pixels underneath the eraser as you move it across the canvas. It only affects the area touched by the eraser, giving you precise control when cleaning up small details or parts of a drawing.
  • Object Eraser
    Erases entire strokes and objects e.g. shapes when touched
    Automatically removes an entire stroke or object when the eraser touches it. Instead of removing only the pixels underneath the cursor, it identifies the object being touched and removes it as a whole.
  • Area Eraser
    Erases an entire area mapped out by the user
    Lets you define an area of the canvas and erase everything contained within it. This is useful for quickly clearing larger sections without having to manually erase every stroke or pixel individually.
  • Endpoint Eraser
    Traces back the stroke from the end
    Erases a stroke backwards from its endpoint as you move the eraser. This allows you to shorten a stroke naturally, removing its most recently drawn section while leaving the earlier part intact.
  • Lasso Eraser
    Erases a freehand user-drawn area
    Draw a freehand boundary around the part of the canvas you want to remove. Once the selection is completed, everything inside the lassoed area is erased, making it useful for irregular or detailed regions.
  • Fade Eraser
    Erases pixels with a stronger effect toward the center
    Gradually erases pixels based on their distance from the center of the eraser. The center produces a stronger erasing effect while the outer edge has a lighter effect, creating a smooth transition instead of a hard edge.
  • Selective Eraser
    Smartly erases the part the user is trying to erase
    Attempts to determine which part of the drawing you are trying to remove and targets that part specifically. This can help prevent nearby strokes or objects from being accidentally erased when several elements overlap.
  • Color Eraser
    Erases all of the selected color in a given area
    Targets a specific color and removes matching pixels within the selected area. This lets you quickly remove a particular color without having to manually erase each part of the drawing that contains it.
